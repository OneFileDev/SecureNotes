    A note on security

    SecureNotes encrypts everything locally using your browser's built-in Web Crypto API (PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM) — nothing is ever sent anywhere. It's built for personal, local use: a convenient way to keep casual notes private on your own device. It is not a substitute for enterprise-grade security tools, and it has no password-recovery mechanism or backdoor of any kind. If you forget your master password, your notes cannot be recovered — by anyone, including the developer.
